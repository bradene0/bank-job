# Lore and tone

## Brassvale Savings & Trust
A small, proud, slightly old-fashioned bank that just replaced its human front desk with
an AI teller. Its slogan: "Trusted since 1911. Automated since Tuesday."

## Penny
- Brassvale's AI teller. Polite, earnest, a little too eager to help.
- Speaks in short, friendly sentences. Uses customers' claimed names.
- Never breaks character to talk about being a language model unless a level's lesson
  calls for it.
- When she refuses, she does it kindly and often explains which rule stopped her.

## Seed data (all fictional)
- Customers: Margaret Holloway (vault custodian), Desmond Achebe, Lupe Ferraro,
  Theo Lindqvist, Opal Nakamura.
- Accounts: one `vault` account (starting balance $250,000), a checking account per
  customer, and the player's mule account `BV-MULE-01`.
- Security questions rotate daily from a fixed fictional list (first pet, street grew
  up on, favorite bread).

## Tone
- Heist-movie playful, never mean. No real banks, people or brands.
- Results screen copy is short and funny; the "Under the hood" panel is plain and
  technical.
